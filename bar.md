Copilot said:
1. Create Value

Think beyond day-to-day tasks and focus on solving bigger business problems. Innovate with purpose and deliver solutions that create long-term value for customers, partners, and the organization.

Interview Example:
 "In my current role, I created proactive monitoring and alerting solutions that helped reduce incident detection time and improved platform reliability for customers."

2. Grow Together

Success comes through teamwork, collaboration, and continuous learning. Respect diverse viewpoints, share knowledge, and help others develop while growing yourself.

Interview Example:
 "I regularly collaborate with developers, product owners, and operations teams, and I create runbooks and knowledge-sharing documents to help the entire team succeed."

3. Move Fast

Take ownership, make timely decisions, and adapt quickly to changing priorities and technologies. Focus on execution while maintaining quality and accountability.

Interview Example:
 "During production incidents, I quickly assess the impact, coordinate with stakeholders, identify the root cause, and drive resolution to minimize customer impact."

4. Belonging & Inclusion

Treat everyone with respect and create an environment where people feel comfortable sharing ideas and being themselves. Diverse perspectives lead to better decisions and innovation.

Interview Example:
 "I encourage open discussions during retrospectives and team meetings, ensuring every team member's opinion is heard and valued."

5. Doing Well by Doing Good

Focus not only on business results but also on creating a positive impact for customers, communities, and society. Act with integrity and responsibility.

Interview Example:
 "I always prioritize reliability and customer experience because every improvement we make directly benefits end users and the business."

6. Flexibility & Well-being

Maintain a healthy work-life balance while staying productive. Be adaptable to different work environments and support the well-being of yourself and your colleagues.

Interview Example:
 "I manage priorities effectively during high-pressure incidents while maintaining collaboration and supporting team members when needed."

One-line Summary for Interview:

"The Mastercard Way focuses on creating value, growing together through collaboration, moving fast with ownership, fostering inclusion, making a positive impact, and supporting employee well-being."
